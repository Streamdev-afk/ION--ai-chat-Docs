ION Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who we are

ION is a chat app where you talk with AI characters. It is run by the ION team. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at streamdevafk@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored securely by our login provider, never in plain text).
  • Your date of birth, which we use to check your age and turn off mature features for under 18s.
  • Your profile: the name you want characters to call you and the short description you write about yourself.
  • Your chats: the messages you send, the replies you receive, reactions, and the notes and facts characters keep about you so they can remember you.
  • Characters you create: their name, backstory, personality settings and prompts.
  • Photos you choose to send in a chat.
  • Your settings and simple usage counters, such as how many messages you sent today.
  • Reports you send us about messages or characters.

How we use your data

  • To run ION: log you in, save your chats, and let characters reply.
  • To keep people safe: check your age, review characters before they are shared, handle reports and block abuse.
  • To keep the service fair: apply daily limits.
  • We do not sell your personal data and we do not show ads.

AI processing by third parties

To write a reply, we send your message, your recent chat, the character's settings, your persona details and any photo you attach to Groq, a third-party AI provider. Groq processes this to generate the reply. Please do not share passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information in a chat.

Characters you share

If you choose to share a character, its name, tagline, backstory, personality, style, skills and quirks become visible to every ION user. Your custom prompt stays private. Your chats with the character are never shared, and we do not show your email or your name. You can remove a shared character at any time.

Service providers

  • Supabase provides our database, login and file storage. Data is stored in the EU West (London) region.
  • Groq provides AI replies, as described above.
  • These providers only process data to provide their service to us.

How long we keep data, and deleting it

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. You can delete a chat, a memory or a character inside the app at any time. You can delete your whole account in Profile > Account > Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, chats, characters, memories and photos. Short-lived backups made by our providers may take a little longer to disappear.

Age requirements

ION is not for children under 13. People aged 13 to 17 get a restricted experience with no mature mode. Mature features are only available to users who are 18 or older according to the date of birth on their account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is restricted so that you can only read your own chats and photos. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices and rights

You can see and edit your profile, turn off chat memory, delete memories, chats, characters and your account inside the app. You can also email streamdevafk@gmail.com to ask for a copy of your data or for help deleting it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: streamdevafk@gmail.com
